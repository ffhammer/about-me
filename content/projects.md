project: Deep Pokis | card
media: projects/deep-pokis_abstract.jpg

media: projects/wg_real.jpg:cover
quote: Probably one of my projects I use and enjoy the most right now.

To make the torturous task of finding a room in Zürich easier, I built this tool.

Also generates WG applications based on your writing, with interactive improvements.
